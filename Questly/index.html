Help

Questly turns your walks into rewarding outdoor adventures. Head outside, open the map, and explore the area around you. Points of interest such as shrines, markets and notice boards appear relative to your GPS position. Materials spawn on the map as you move. Tap them when you're within 200m to collect. Complete quests to earn coins. Use coins at the market to buy rare items. Each archetype has two affinity terrains. Materials found there appear 3× more often for you. The landscape changes what spawns: forest at night yields Nightbloom, storms bring Stormstone.

Five types of location appear around you: Shrines (purple) for daily quests and trading items, Markets (amber) to buy and sell with coins, Quest Boards (green) to browse and start field quests, the Scholar (teal) to buy knowledge scrolls, and Wilderness Points (dark green) for exploration. You need to be within 80–100m of a location to interact. Tap any marker to see how far you are and how long the walk takes.

Materials appear as coloured circles on the map. White-bordered green = common. Amber-bordered = rare. Purple-bordered = legendary. Legendary materials pulse when you're within 200m. Tap any marker to see what it is when you're in range. Out of range, it stays hidden. You can hold no more than 20 of any one material. Crafted items and special finds (Nightbloom, Stormstone) don't count toward your inventory cap. Check the Field Guide to learn which materials appear in which terrain and for which archetype.

Tap the quest bar at the top of the map to browse available quests. Quests come in easy, medium and hard difficulty. Each quest has a clear objective: collect a number of materials, walk a distance, or visit shrines. Completing a quest awards XP and coins. Daily quests track automatically. Just walk and collect. Field quests require you to walk to a pin on the map to unlock. Your archetype may earn bonus coins on certain quests. Only one field quest can be active at a time, but daily quests always run in parallel.

Your archetype shapes what you find and how you interact with the world. Each archetype has two affinity terrains. Materials found in those terrains appear more often for you. Archetypes also give quest bonuses: certain quests award extra coins if your archetype matches. Your archetype is permanent. Choose carefully on first open. The Alchemist finds fungi and resins. The Blacksmith finds ore and metal. The Beastmaster finds feathers and tracks. The Cartographer finds stones and river markers.

Markets appear on the map as amber markers. You must be within 80m to trade. At the market you can spend coins to buy rare and legendary materials you haven't found yet. Coin prices reflect rarity: rare items cost more than common ones, legendary items are expensive. Earn coins by completing quests. Your coin balance is shown in the amber pill on the map topbar. The Craft tab lets you combine common materials into rare ones without spending coins, but only if your archetype has access. Alchemists can brew Reveal Potions. Blacksmiths can forge Keys. The Sell tab lets you list materials others can buy. Prices are fixed by rarity: common 10 coins, rare 30, legendary 100.
